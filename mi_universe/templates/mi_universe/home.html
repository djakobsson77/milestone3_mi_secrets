Welcome to the Monkey Island Universe

What is the secret of Monkey Island?

First of all, it is an adventure game that was very popular during the 1990s. All who were playing much computer games during the 90's for sure knows this game or have at least heard about it. You can read more in detail about the game in the about section.

But what is the SECRET of Monkey Island?

Do you really think that I would reveal the secret right away? No, but ask yourself... is there really a secret about Monkey Island? And, is it really revealed?
I have myself played all the games and explored every corner of this island and other islands as well, and the answer is still very unclear.
But I won't say more than that about it because it would be a spoiler and it would ruin the whole experience.

I invite you to explore the islands and discover the secrets for yourself and this website will help you a little to get to know the games better.
