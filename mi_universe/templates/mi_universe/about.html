The secret of Monkey Island™

This is the first scene when the game begins... Classic and iconic, isn't it? The game starts with a pirate wannabe, Guybrush Threepwood, and according to the story, he was washed ashore at the island of Mêlée. He is eager to become a pirate and sets out on a quest to prove himself. Along the way, he encounters various characters, solves puzzles, and uncovers the secrets of Monkey Island™. The game is known for its humor, engaging storyline, and memorable characters. It's a beloved classic that has captivated players for decades.

The secret of Monkey Island™ game series

First of all, it is a series of adventure games that was very popular during the 1990s. All who were playing much computer games during the 90's for sure knows this game or have at least heard about it.

In my personal opinion, this is one of my absolute favorite adventure games developed and published by the marvelous LucasArts™ computergame company. I really like even the first release from 1990 with not so fancy graphics but it's amazing soundtrack in it and the dialogues are razorsharp and many times with great humor. You get this cozy atmosphere and I can really recommend this game to everyone who haven't played this before. Especially since the game programmer and designer Ron Gilbert did a fantastic job and happened to make the game to what it became.
However, many years later Lucasarts released a remake of the game with updated graphics and sound and many prefer this one. Ron Gilbert was involved also in the sequel, LeChuck's revenge™. But, even if the third game in the series, The curse of Monkey Island™ was not a Ron Gilbert production, it is actually a worthy followup on the sequel with very good graphics and sound. But after this, the 4th (Escape from Monkey Island™) and 5th (Tales of Monkey Island™) parts was weak followups and it never reach the standard of the three first but they are still are enjoyable games.
As late as 2022 a 6th part of the game was released (Return to Monkey Island™) and this time, Ron Gilbert was back and involved in the development of the game. It was sure better than the 4th and 5th game but still not like the old ones. But... it had a great soundtrack and had it's nice moments.

And let's return to the secret of Monkey Island™? Well, I will not spoil it for you. You have to play the game to find out. But I can say that it is a very good story and you will really enjoy the journey. So, if you want to find out the secret of Monkey Island, you have to play the game.
